# archive/

Finished or abandoned projects, at the same sub-path they had before (`archive/games/roblox/farm-game/`). They arrive
here through `/archive-project`.

- Archived projects are read-only. To work on one again, `/promote` it back to its category first.
- Its `projects.json` entry stays, with status `archived` and the new path.
- Its GitHub repo may have moved to the archive org (`AGENTS.md` → Names and owners); the entry's `owner` says so.
